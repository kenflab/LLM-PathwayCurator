Dexamethasone source checks and descriptive human review

DEX_text_checks_50.tsv contains all 50 unchanged English model outputs, source NES and BH q, text hashes, and saved rule findings. DEX_case_ratings_24.tsv and DEX_returned_categories_48.tsv preserve returned categories for 24 texts assessed by two evaluators. J1 / AUTHOR_J1 denotes the author; J2 / AUTHOR_J2 denotes the external evaluator. Evaluator names, free-text rationales, correspondence, local paths and response timestamps are not included.

DEX_review_sources_24.tsv retains the source records for the selected review cases. DEX_review_definitions.json documents the rubric definitions. Instructions were in Japanese; the model texts were English. Rubric translation equivalence was not formally assessed. Ratings were not adjudicated or recoded. Missing hypothesis usefulness is conditional, not a negative judgment.

The 50 texts informed rule development. The 24-text subset is selected and the author was exposed to development material. Counts and agreement are descriptive; these files do not support a pooled human reference, sensitivity/specificity or a comparative accuracy estimate.
