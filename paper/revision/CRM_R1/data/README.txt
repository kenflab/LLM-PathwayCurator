Supplementary datasets for LLM-PathwayCurator

Data 1: TCGA cohort accounting, all 350 Hallmark records, q-only comparison, HNSC overlap, and provenance (Figure 2).
Data 2: 50 unchanged dexamethasone model texts, source checks, 24 review cases, anonymized returned categories, rubric definitions, and summaries (Figure 3).
Data 3: A separate three-evaluator review of 50 fixed HNSC statements, with category counts and agreement.
Data 4: Separate selection-replication and GO/Reactome hierarchy comparisons.

Recorded configuration labels and historical filenames are retained for provenance. For example, figure2_* in Data 4 refers to the separate replication analysis, not the current main Figure 2. Analysis configurations and statement corpora are not interchangeable. Historical filenames and local provenance paths do not imply that every referenced intermediate is included. EXPORT_NOTES.json records portable path-prefix substitutions in two manifests; result values and recorded hashes are unchanged.

The human-review exports contain anonymized categorical responses. Private correspondence, evaluator names, and free-text rationales are not included. These ratings are descriptive and do not provide a pooled biological accuracy reference. No human labels were recoded.

Execution versions are specified in the manuscript and per-analysis records. The figure-rendering and source-checking snapshot is d0af5d74f4276f2a05cdcb926d076e1a8cd8593f. Rendering saved tables does not repeat the upstream statistical analyses or validate biological accuracy.
